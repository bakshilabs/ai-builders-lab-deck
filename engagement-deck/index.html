What we know

From our 30 September meeting and the ComputerXplorers website

  • Six hours of learning for 10 to 11 year olds
  • A half-term block or six weekly one-hour sessions
  • Around 15 children in a class
  • A non-specialist instructor must be able to run it
  • Children should see, question and change what AI does
  • School firewalls and children's data matter from day one
  • Views on AI differ inside ComputerXplorers
What we have built

In the AI Builders Lab prototype, today

  • Four maths and science builds, one per interest
  • A unit of six one-hour sessions, ending in a product to share
  • A five-step prompt ladder, and an AI that highlights what it guessed
  • Session plans and an example AI that works offline
  • A mock-up of an instructor hub on Circle
